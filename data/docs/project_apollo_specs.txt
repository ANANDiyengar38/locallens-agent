# Project Apollo - Confidential Internal Specifications (2026)

## 1. Project Overview
Project Apollo is our internal next-generation autonomous drone platform designed for environmental inspection and search-and-rescue operations.
Lead Engineer: Dr. Samantha Vance
Project Budget: $4.2 Million
Launch Target: Q3 2026

## 2. Technical Hardware Architecture
- Power Unit: Dual Solid-State Lithium batteries providing up to 90 minutes of continuous flight time.
- Processing Unit: Embedded Jetson Orin Nano running a lightweight quantized vision model.
- Communications: Mesh Wi-Fi 7 with fall-back satellite uplink (Iridium network).
- Operating Temperature: Rated between -20°C and 55°C.

## 3. Compliance and Safety Guidelines
- Maximum allowed autonomous altitude: 120 meters (400 feet) above ground level.
- Geofencing: Hardcoded no-fly zones around all Category A public airports within a 15 km radius.
- Fail-safe trigger: Automatic return-to-base (RTB) initiates when battery level drops below 18%.
